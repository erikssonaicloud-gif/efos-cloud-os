  * **Findings:** All 12 claimed scene prefixes (`1994–2024`) matched exact USGS Collection 2 Level-2 scenes (`11` Tier 1 scenes; `2010-08-20` is Tier 2 `T2`). Every Chapin Court Land Surface Temperature (`LST`), every two-control average (`Rivercrest` + `Hillendale`), and every thermal excess value reproduced within `±0.005°F` (exact to the published hundredth of a degree). All 36 `QA_PIXEL` samples have bits 0–5 unset (clear sky) and `QA_RADSAT = 0`. Discloses that on 9 of 12 dates, individual point-sample control extractions differ from the published single-control columns by equal and opposite offsets (up to `3.99°F` in 2010) while yielding the identical two-control mean. Exploratory two-sided Welch test between `<90°F` (`+10.99°F`, `n=7`) and `≥90°F` (`+12.86°F`, `n=5`) yields `p = 0.26605`.
